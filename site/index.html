Mental model

CSS is not positioning instructions

Most declarations feed constraints into a layout algorithm. The final coordinates are an output of several stages, not values authored directly in the stylesheet.

  1. StyleResolve selectors, inheritance, cascade, and computed values.
  2. Box generationTurn the element tree into the boxes participating in layout.
  3. LayoutResolve sizes and positions under the chosen formatting contexts.
  4. Paint & compositeTurn geometry into visual layers, pixels, and composited output.
